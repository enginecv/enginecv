## The Problem With “Free” Resume Builders

If you’ve ever searched **“free resume builder”**, you already know what happens.

You click one of the top results — the ones dominating SEO rankings — and everything looks great.

- Clean UI  
- Smooth experience  
- “100% free” claims  

So you start building.

You spend **20–30 minutes** crafting your resume.

Then…

> **Download as PDF → Premium only**

---

## The Sunk Cost Trap

This isn’t bad UX.

It’s intentional.

It’s called the **sunk cost fallacy**.

You’ve already invested time, so paying feels easier than starting over.

And yeah — it works.

It happened to me **three separate times** on different sites.

Each time I thought:

> “Okay, this one won’t do that.”

Every time — same outcome.

So eventually:

> **“I’ll just build my own.”**

---

## A Resume Isn’t Your Life Story

Another issue most builders ignore:

A CV isn’t supposed to be your entire life story anymore.

Recruiters don’t care about everything you’ve ever done.

They care about one thing:

> **Are you a fit for this specific job?**

Sending the same resume everywhere is one of the biggest mistakes people make.

You need:

- Tailoring  
- Rewriting  
- Role-specific focus  

Most builders don’t help with that.  
They just format text.

---

## The Bigger Problem: Your Data

Now the real issue.

When you use an online resume builder, you’re giving away:

- Full name  
- Address  
- Work history  
- Education  
- Timeline of your life  

That’s extremely sensitive data.

And most platforms:

- Store it on their servers  
- Push you into OAuth signups  
- Use it for retention and marketing  

Personally, I don’t want my entire identity sitting on some random server.

## What It Deliberately Does Not Do

- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for or log in to.
- **No sync.** Your CV does not travel between devices on its own.
- **No server copy.** Nobody, including me, can restore a CV you have lost.
- **No AI features yet.** See the FAQ below.

Each of these is a trade-off. They are what keeps your data on your device, and they are also the reason you need to look after your own backups.

---

## Where Your Data Lives, and the Catch

Your CVs are saved in your browser's local database, on your device. That is what keeps them private, and it is also the catch.

If you clear your site data, switch browsers or lose the device without having exported, the CVs are gone, because there is no server copy to restore from. Export a JSON backup after any edit that matters, and before you clear your browser data.

The **Your data** page shows what EngineCV stores in your browser, lets you download a copy of it and lets you delete all of it. It is the place to go if you want to check the claims on this page for yourself.

---

## How to Back Up Your CV

Because there is no server copy, a backup is something you do yourself. It takes under a minute.

1. Open your CV and export it as JSON.
2. Keep the file somewhere you trust, such as a cloud drive or a USB stick.
3. To restore it, or to move to another device, import the JSON file.

If the backup contains personal details and you will store it somewhere shared, use the encrypted JSON export. Keep the password safe, because there is no server that holds a copy of it.

## A Few Honest Limits

Because everything runs in your browser, EngineCV is only as good as your browser. The PDF export uses your browser's own print engine, so check the file you are about to send, including page breaks and links, before you submit it.

It is also a young project. If something does not work the way this page says, tell me, because that is a bug and not a feature.
